- **Finding:** across pharmacy apps, the **#1 complaint is delay or cancellation, at 16–29% of bad reviews**. Support (15–22%) and refunds (7–13%) come next.
- Fees and price complaints are small (1–6%).
- Prescription-upload complaints are rare by keyword (0–2%). But real quotes describe the annoyance: PharmEasy, "unnecessary phone calls, even after uploading prescription"; Apollo, "uploaded genuine… prescription… they hold the order and no one is responding".
- **For labs:** Healthians' bad reviews are dominated by sample/phlebotomist problems (21.8%) and support (23.6%).
- **What this means [J]:** users don't mainly lose on price. **They lose after paying: the order is late, cancelled or out of stock, and nobody helps.** A neutral app that can **switch to another seller automatically** fixes the biggest pain.

## 3. Our identity

**Parchi is on the patient's side of the prescription.**
1. One photo gets every medicine *and* every test on it.
2. You see the nearest real chemist, lab, Jan Aushadhi store, 10-minute app and online pharmacy, each with the **true final price**.
3. Parchi places the order or booking for you. **If it fails, Parchi switches to the next-best option automatically.**

| They do badly (evidence) | We do well |
|---|---|
| Sellers only show **their own** catalogue and substitutes (1mg, PharmEasy, Truemeds) [V] | Neutral: we compare everyone, including local shops |
| Comparers cover **medicines only** (QuickPill, MediSaathi, Davaio) or **tests only** (LabFare, Smart Bharat) [V] | The whole parchi in one plan |
| No comparer covers **nearby real shops and labs** or **10-minute apps** [V] | Real nearby places (phase 4) plus Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart (phase 5) |
| **16–29% of bad reviews** are about delays or cancellations, and nobody re-routes the order [M] | **Plan B:** if an order is cancelled, out of stock or late, we re-order from the next-best seller, with your one-tap OK |
| Prescriptions get **checked by hand and confirmed by phone call** (1mg, PharmEasy) [V] | Reads in seconds, on the phone, including Hindi prescriptions; only doubtful lines come back to you (phase 1) |
| Comparers are **websites only** (QuickPill, MediSaathi, Davaio) [V] | A native iPhone app: camera scanner, share-from-WhatsApp, reminders |
| Comparers **send you elsewhere to buy** (Davaio, MedScanner) | One-tap order or booking, with a manual option if you prefer |

## 4. Techniques borrowed from other fields
1. **Payment smart routing:**
   - Juspay picks a payment gateway per order based on success rates, and automatically routes to a healthy backup gateway during an outage [V: Juspay docs; its uplift numbers are vendor claims].
   - **For Parchi:** route each medicine or test to the best seller; if it fails, re-route to the next one = **Plan B**.
2. **GoodRx (US):** compares cash prices at nearby pharmacies and earns from coupon fees, a subscription and ads [V]. **For Parchi:** shows that comparing local chemists works and earns money. India has no equivalent covering both shops and labs [J].

## 5. End-of-phase report

**Consolidated idea:**
1. Snap or share your parchi.
2. Parchi reads every medicine and test, with your own model and an on-phone check.
3. It builds one plan: the cheapest *complete* option across nearby chemists and labs, Jan Aushadhi, 10-minute apps and online pharmacies, with the true final price.
4. One tap orders and books.
5. If anything fails, Plan B re-routes automatically.

**Novelty line:**
- **Tier 1 candidate:** cross-seller Plan B (payment-style failover applied to medicines and lab tests), plus whole-parchi planning that covers offline and online together. Not found in any of the 15 players.
- Claim it as "no public product found".

**Innovation line (tier 2):**
- **Today:** QuickPill and MediSaathi compare **online medicine prices only**, and LabFare compares tests only.
- **Parchi adds:** tests, nearby real places, 10-minute apps, the true final price, one-tap action and automatic Plan B.

**Automations (5 from phase 1, 5 new, 10 total):**
- AU6 compare across every channel
- AU7 calculate the true final price (fees, minimum order)
- AU8 Plan B re-route on cancel, out-of-stock or delay
- AU9 one-tap order (or manual hand-off)
- AU10 lab booking with a confirmation number

**Need → why:**
