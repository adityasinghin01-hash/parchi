# Parchi survey kit (Google Form) — closes the HackIIITD "no user evidence" gap
**Target:** 20–30 answers by tomorrow 12:00 (KIET students + their families). Send it as ONE WhatsApp message to class and family groups.
**Honest label in the deck:** "Convenience sample, n = X, KIET students and families. Not representative of India."

## Form questions (copy into Google Forms; ~2 minutes; Hindi in brackets)
1. Your age group: under 25 / 25–44 / 45–59 / 60+
2. Who did you last buy medicines for? Myself / Parent or grandparent / Child / Other  (आख़िरी बार दवा किसके लिए ली?)
3. After your last doctor visit, where did you buy the medicines? (tick all) Doctor's own or clinic chemist / Nearby chemist / Online app (1mg, PharmEasy, Apollo…) / 10-minute app (Blinkit, Zepto…) / Jan Aushadhi / Didn't buy
4. Did you compare prices anywhere before buying? Yes / No, didn't think of it / No, didn't know how
5. Could you read every medicine on the parchi yourself? All / Some / None  (क्या आप पर्ची पर लिखी हर दवा पढ़ पाए?)
6. Were any tests written on it (blood test, X-ray…)? Yes / No
7. If yes: how did you choose the lab? Doctor told me / Nearest / Cheapest / An app / Didn't get tests done
8. In total, how many shops, labs or apps did it take to get everything done? 1 / 2 / 3 / 4+
9. Has an online medicine or lab order of yours ever been late or cancelled? Never / Once / More than once / Never ordered online
10. If yes, what did you do? (short answer)
11. Did you know Jan Aushadhi medicines with the same salt often cost far less? Yes / No
12. Would you ask your doctor before switching to a cheaper same-salt medicine? Yes / No / Not sure
13. How likely are you to use an app that reads your parchi and shows the cheapest complete way to get all medicines AND tests, with the full price upfront? 1 (not at all) – 5 (very likely)
14. What would stop you from using it? (short answer)
15. OK to be contacted for a 10-minute app test tomorrow? Yes (phone number) / No

**Privacy line at the top of the form:** "No prescription photos. Answers are used only for our hackathon project and deleted after the event."
