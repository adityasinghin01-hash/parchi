## Journeys (each as an XCUITest where it is stable, plus screenshots `app/verify/k24-<journey>-<step>.png`)
1. First-time user, Mock: Home, consent (read it), a photo, preview, Confirm, Plan List, Plan Map, shop sheet, Get it all, Track, Simulate cancel, Plan B, Care, History, Settings, Delete everything, back to Home.
2. Typed entry only: add 3 medicines (one H1 like Alprax), 1 test, remove one, change a strength, continue; check every message makes sense to a non-technical person.
3. Edge parchis: empty list; every line "ask my doctor"; 20 lines; a 60-character medicine name; quantity 1 and 1000; Hindi/emoji text typed into the name field; a medicine with no price.
4. Live mode with the server: shop ask (3 shops), lab booking, Plan B via judge cancel.
5. Bad conditions: server down in the middle of a Live order; Delete everything while a sheet is open; background and relaunch during Track; two quick taps on every primary button (no double order, no double sheet).
## Environments (every screen of journey 1, in each)
Dynamic Type at the largest standard size and at an accessibility size (AX3/AX5); dark mode; Reduce Motion; Bold Text; iPhone SE (3rd generation) and iPhone 17 Pro Max simulators (create them, delete them at the end). Look for: clipped or overlapping text, buttons under 44 pt or covered by the bottom bar, white-on-white or low contrast, content you cannot scroll to, dead ends (a screen with no way back), jargon ("Live", "Mock", "sandbox", "Temporal", "paise") shown to a normal user, English-only error messages with no next step.
## Programmatic checks
`try app.performAccessibilityAudit()` (XCTest, Xcode 26) on each main screen: record every issue type and element; fix those that are cheap (missing label, tap target size, contrast); list the rest.
